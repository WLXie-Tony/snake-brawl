蛇蛇乱斗 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日

蛇蛇乱斗（下称"本游戏"，包括 iOS 版、Android 版和网页版）由 Wenlan Xie 个人开发。这份说明讲清楚本游戏会用到哪些信息、为什么用、去了哪里，以及你可以怎么关掉或删除。

不用注册

本游戏不需要你注册，不会要你填写手机号、邮箱或真实姓名。第一次打开时，游戏会自动生成一个匿名账号（一串随机编号），用来保存云存档和上排行榜，它和你的真实身份没有关联。

保存在开发者服务器上的数据

服务器托管在 Cloudflare。上面会保存：

  • 云存档：昵称、等级、金币、拥有的物品、外观设计和设置，用来在换手机时找回进度。
  • 游玩统计：一个随机的设备编号，以及开局、结束、死亡原因、领取奖励、看广告、购买等游戏内事件，还有设备类型、屏幕大小、语言、游戏版本，以及 Cloudflare 根据网络地址判断的国家或地区。这些数据里不包含你的 IP 地址；服务器只为防止恶意刷请求短暂记录 IP，最多一天后删除。统计用来改进游戏，保留 180 天后自动删除。你可以在游戏的"账号"页里随时关掉统计。
  • 每周排行榜：你的昵称、皮肤和本周最好成绩，所有玩家都能看到。成绩保留 8 周。

在"账号"页点"删除账号"，会删除你的云存档、统计记录和排行榜成绩；你也可以发邮件让我们删除。

联机时会发生什么

  • 联机时，你的设备会尽量和同房间其他玩家的设备直接连接（点对点）。对方会看到你的昵称、蛇的外观和你在游戏里的动作，也能看到你的网络地址（IP 地址）。
  • 为了让设备找到彼此，游戏使用开发者在 Cloudflare 上的配对服务器；在没有配置这台服务器的版本里，使用 PeerJS 的公共配对服务器（0.peerjs.com）。配对服务器只在建立连接时看到你的 IP 地址，不保存游戏数据。
  • 如果两台设备无法直接连接，游戏数据会经过 Cloudflare 的中继服务器转发。中继服务器只转发数据，不读取也不保存。
  • "在线匹配"会把你放进公开房间，房间里的陌生玩家能看到你的昵称和外观。

广告（只在 App 里）

  • App 用 Google AdMob 显示广告：看广告领奖励是你自己选择的，另外在几局之间可能出现全屏广告（买过任何东西或"去广告"后不再出现）。
  • AdMob 会收集设备的广告标识、IP 地址（用来估计大概位置）、广告的展示和点击、App 的性能和崩溃信息，用于投放和统计广告、防止作弊。详见 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。
  • 在欧洲经济区和英国，第一次显示广告前会先问你是否同意个性化广告，以后可以在商店页的"广告隐私设置"里修改。在 iPhone 上，只有你在系统弹窗里允许后，广告才会用到设备的广告标识；不允许也能玩全部内容。

购买（只在 App 里）

  • 购买由 App Store 或 Google Play 处理，开发者看不到你的付款方式。
  • 为了确认购买有效、恢复购买，购买记录和你的匿名账号编号会交给 RevenueCat 处理。详见 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

提醒通知（只在 App 里）

如果你允许，App 会在你的设备上安排每日礼物等提醒。这些提醒在你的手机上生成，不经过服务器。你可以在系统设置里关掉。

其他

  • 游戏的字体从 Google Fonts 加载，Google 因此会看到你的 IP 地址。
  • 以上第三方服务如何处理数据，以它们各自的隐私政策为准。

儿童

本游戏不是专门面向 13 岁以下儿童设计的，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为孩子的信息被收集了，请联系我们删除。

Snake Brawl Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Snake Brawl (蛇蛇乱斗, "the game", including the iOS, Android and web versions) is made by an independent developer, Wenlan Xie. This page explains what information the game uses, why, where it goes, and how you can turn it off or delete it.

No sign-up

The game never asks you to sign up, and never asks for your phone number, email or real name. The first time you open it, the game creates an anonymous account (a random ID) for your cloud save and the leaderboards. It is not linked to who you are.

Data kept on the developer's server

The server is hosted by Cloudflare. It keeps:

  • Cloud save: your nickname, level, coins, items, skin designs and settings, so you can move your progress to a new phone.
  • Play statistics: a random device ID and in-game events such as matches started and finished, causes of death, rewards claimed, ads watched and purchases made, plus device type, screen size, language, game version, and the country or region Cloudflare works out from your network address. None of this includes your IP address; the server only holds IP addresses for up to a day to stop floods of requests. Statistics help improve the game and are deleted after 180 days. You can switch them off at any time on the game's Account page.
  • Weekly leaderboards: your nickname, skin and best score of the week, visible to all players. Scores are kept for 8 weeks.

"Delete account" on the Account page deletes your cloud save, statistics and leaderboard scores. You can also email us to delete them.

When you play online

  • In a room, your device connects directly to the other players' devices when it can (peer to peer). They see your nickname, your snake's look and your moves, and their devices can see your network (IP) address.
  • To help devices find each other, the game uses the developer's matchmaking server on Cloudflare; versions without that server use the public PeerJS server (0.peerjs.com). The matchmaking server sees your IP address while a connection is set up and does not keep game data.
  • When two devices cannot connect directly, game traffic goes through Cloudflare's relay servers, which pass it on without reading or storing it.
  • Play online puts you in public rooms, where players you don't know can see your nickname and look.

Ads (apps only)

  • The apps show ads with Google AdMob. Ads that give rewards are your choice; full-screen ads may also appear between matches (they stop after any purchase, or with No ads).
  • AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate a general location), which ads you saw and tapped, and app performance and crash information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent before the first ad, and you can change your choice later under "Ad privacy settings" in the shop. On iPhone, ads use your device's advertising ID only if you allow it in the system prompt; you can say no and still play everything.

Purchases (apps only)

  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.
  • To check that purchases are valid and to restore them, purchase records and your anonymous account ID are processed by RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Reminders (apps only)

If you allow it, the app schedules reminders such as your daily gift on your device. They are created on your phone and never go through a server. You can turn them off in your system settings.

Other

  • The game's fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address.
  • How these third-party services handle data is covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.
